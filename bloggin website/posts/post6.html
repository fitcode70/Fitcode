Chicken Keema Rice Bowl

🍱 Lunch meal 📆 09-jun-2026 💪 high protein
Nutrition (Approx.)

Calories: 750–850 kcal
Protein: 45–55g
Carbs: 80–90g
Fats: 18–22g

A flavorful lean bulking lunch packed with protein

ingredients
150g Lean Ground Chicken (or Chicken Mince chest peice of chicken), 200g Cooked Rice, 100g Green Peas, 1 Medium Onion (Chopped), 1 Medium Tomato (Chopped), 1 tbsp Olive Oil, 1 tsp Garlic Paste, Salt, Black Pepper, Chili Powder.

Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté onion until golden.
2. Add garlic paste and chopped tomato. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
3. Add the chicken mince and cook until browned.
4. Stir in green peas, salt, pepper, and chili powder.
5. Cook for another 5 minutes until everything is well combined.
6. Serve the chicken and pea mixture over freshly cooked rice.
7. Enjoy while hot.

Why It's Great for Lean Bulking
✅ High protein content promotes muscle growth and recovery
✅ Rice provides workout-fueling carbohydrates
✅ Green peas add fiber and extra protein
✅ Lean chicken keeps fats under control
✅ Budget-friendly and meal-prep friendly
