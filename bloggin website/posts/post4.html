Paneer Egg Power Toast

🍱 break-fast 📆 08-jun-2026 💪 high protein
Nutrition (Approx.)

Calories: 700–800 kcal
Protein: 45–50g
Carbs: 70–80g
Fats: 20–25g

A powerful breakfast packed with protein and calories.

ingredients
4 Whole Eggs, 4 Slices Whole Wheat Bread, 100g Paneer (Crumbled), 1 tbsp Butter, 1 Medium Tomato (Chopped), 1 Small Onion (Chopped), Salt, Black Pepper, Chili Flakes, 250ml Milk.

Instructions
1. Heat butter in a pan and sauté onion and tomato for 2–3 minutes.
2. Add crumbled paneer, salt, pepper, and chili flakes. Cook for 3 minutes.
3. Beat the eggs and pour them into the pan.
4. Stir gently until the eggs are fully cooked and mixed with the paneer.
5. Toast the whole wheat bread until golden brown.
6. Serve the paneer egg scramble with toast and a glass of milk.

Why It's Great for Lean Bulking
✅ Combines complete protein from eggs with extra protein from paneer
✅ High-calorie meal ideal for weight and muscle gain
✅ Rich in healthy fats and essential nutrients
✅ Provides long-lasting energy for workouts and daily activities
✅ Easy to prepare with simple ingredients
