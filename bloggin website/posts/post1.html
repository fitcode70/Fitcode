High-Protein Bulking Chicken Rice Bowl

🍱 Lunch 📆 08-jun-2026 💪 high protein
Nutrition (Approx.)

Calories: 850–950 kcal
Protein: 60–70grams
Carbs: 85–95g
Fat: 20–25g

Perfect for muscle gain and clean bulking.

ingredients
250g chicken breast. 2 cups cooked rice. 1 cup broccoli. 1 tbsp olive oil. 1 tbsp soy sauce. 1 tsp garlic powder. 1 tsp paprika. Salt & black pepper.
Optional Bulking Boost.
2 boiled eggs. 1 tbsp peanut butter. 1 glass milk.

Instructions
Step 1
Season chicken with: Salt, Black pepper, Paprika, and Garlic powder.
Step 2
Heat olive oil in a pan and cook chicken for 6–8 minutes per side until golden.
Step 3
Steam or sauté broccoli.
Step 4
Place rice in a bowl and top with chicken and broccoli.
Step 5
Drizzle soy sauce and serve.

Bulking Tip
For hard gainers: ✅ Add 2 eggs (+12g protein) ✅ Add 1 glass milk (+8g protein) ✅ Add 1 banana on the side This can push the meal close to: 1100+ calories and 80g+ protein in a single meal.
