Paneer Curry with Rice

🍱 Dinner Meal 📆 08-jun-2026 💪 high protein
Nutrition (Approx.)

Calories: 850–950 kcal
Protein: 42–48g
Carbs: 100–110g
Fats: 22–28g

A simple and comforting curry rice meal that's rich in protein and calories,

ingredients
200g Paneer (Cubed), 300g Cooked Rice, 1 Medium Onion (Finely Chopped), 2 Medium Tomatoes (Pureed), 2 tbsp Curd, 1 tbsp Oil, 1 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, ½ tsp Turmeric Powder, 1 tsp Coriander Powder, ½ tsp Red Chili Powder, ½ tsp Garam Masala, Salt, Fresh Coriander.

Instructions
1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the chopped onion until golden.
2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add tomato puree and cook until the oil starts separating.
4. Mix in turmeric, coriander powder, chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
5. Add curd and stir well to create a creamy curry base.
6. Add paneer cubes and cook for 4–5 minutes until coated with the curry.
7. Simmer for 3 minutes and garnish with fresh coriander.
8. Serve the hot paneer curry with cooked rice.
9. Mix and enjoy a calorie-dense bulking dinner.

Why It's Great for Lean Bulking
✅ Over 40g of high-quality protein for muscle recovery
✅ Rice provides long-lasting energy and workout fuel
✅ Paneer supplies slow-digesting casein protein
✅ High-calorie meal ideal for healthy weight gain
✅ Easy to prepare with simple ingredients
