Soya Chunks Curry Rice

🍱 Dinner Meal 📆 08-jun-2026 🥦 vegetarian
Nutrition (Approx.)

Calories: 850–950 kcal
Protein: 50–55g
Carbs: 95–105g
Fats: 18–22g

A high-protein soya chunks curry served with rice, perfect for vegetarians looking to gain muscle

ingredients
100g Soya Chunks (Dry), 300g Cooked Rice, 1 Medium Onion (Finely Chopped), 2 Medium Tomatoes (Pureed), 2 tbsp Curd, 1 tbsp Oil, 1 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, ½ tsp Turmeric Powder, 1 tsp Coriander Powder, ½ tsp Red Chili Powder, ½ tsp Garam Masala, Salt, Fresh Coriander.

Instructions
1. Boil the soya chunks for 5–7 minutes and squeeze out excess water.
2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the chopped onion until golden brown.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add tomato puree and cook until thick.
5. Mix in turmeric, coriander powder, chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
6. Add curd and stir well to make a rich curry base.
7. Add the boiled soya chunks and mix thoroughly.
8. Simmer for 5–7 minutes so the chunks absorb the curry flavors.
9. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with cooked rice.

Why It's Great for Lean Bulking
✅ Over 50g of plant-based protein for muscle growth
✅ Budget-friendly alternative to chicken and paneer
✅ Rice provides energy for intense workouts
✅ Low in fat while remaining calorie-dense
✅ High satiety and easy meal prep option
