Soya Protein Scramble with Toast

🍱 break-fast 📆 08-jun-2026 🥦 vegetarian
Nutrition (Approx.)

Calories: 600–700 kcal
Protein: 35–40g
Carbs: 70–80g
Fats: 15–20g

A simple Indian-style breakfast packed with protein and calories,

ingredients
100g Soya Chunks, 4 Slices Whole Wheat Bread, 1 Medium Onion (Chopped), 1 Medium Tomato (Chopped), 1 tsp Oil, Salt, Black Pepper, Chili Flakes, 250ml Milk.

Instructions
1. Boil the soya chunks for 5 minutes and squeeze out excess water.
2. Chop the soya chunks into small pieces.
3. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onion and tomato until soft.
4. Add the soya chunks, salt, pepper, and chili flakes.
5. Cook for 4–5 minutes while stirring occasionally.
6. Toast the bread slices until golden brown.
7. Serve the soya scramble with toast and a glass of milk.

Why It's Great for Lean Bulking
✅ Soya chunks provide a large amount of affordable protein
✅ Great alternative to expensive meat-based breakfasts
✅ High in complex carbohydrates for sustained energy
✅ Supports muscle recovery and growth
✅ Uses inexpensive ingredients available in most grocery stores
