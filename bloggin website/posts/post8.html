Paneer Besan Chilla

🍱 Lunch Meal 📆 08-jun-2026 🥦 vegetarian
Nutrition (Approx.)

Calories: 700–800 kcal
Protein: 40–45g
Carbs: 75–85g
Fats: 18–22g

A high-protein vegetarian lunch that is easy to make, budget-friendly

ingredients
100g Besan (Gram Flour), 100g Paneer (Grated), 1 Small Onion (Finely Chopped), 1 Small Tomato (Finely Chopped), 1 Green Chili (Optional), 1 tsp Cumin Seeds, Salt, Water as Needed, 1 tbsp Oil, 200g Curd (Yogurt).

Instructions
1. In a bowl, mix besan, onion, tomato, cumin seeds, chili, and salt.
2. Add water gradually to make a smooth batter.
3. Heat a non-stick pan and lightly grease it with oil.
4. Pour the batter and spread it into a thin chilla.
5. Sprinkle grated paneer evenly over the chilla.
6. Cook for 2–3 minutes on each side until golden brown.
7. Serve hot with fresh curd on the side.

Why It's Great for Lean Bulking
✅ High in protein from besan, paneer, and curd
✅ Easy to digest and not overly heavy
✅ Provides steady energy from complex carbohydrates
✅ Budget-friendly and made with simple ingredients
✅ Great for meal prep and quick lunches
