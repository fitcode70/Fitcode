Chicken Protein Fried Rice

🍱 Dinner Meal 📆 08-jun-2026 💪 high protein
Nutrition (Approx.)

Calories: 850–950 kcal
Protein: 50–55g
Carbs: 90–100g
Fats: 25–30g

A protein-packed bulking dinner designed to maximize muscle recovery and growth.

ingredients
200g Chicken Breast (Boneless), 250g Cooked Rice, 2 Whole Eggs, 1 tbsp Olive Oil, 1 Medium Onion (Chopped), 1 Medium Tomato (Chopped), 1 tsp Garlic Paste, Salt, Black Pepper, Chili Powder, Fresh Coriander.

Instructions
1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and chili powder.
2. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the chicken until golden and fully cooked.
3. Remove the chicken and cut it into bite-sized pieces.
4. In the same pan, sauté onion and garlic until fragrant.
5. Add tomato and cook until soft.
6. Add the cooked rice and mix well.
7. Crack in the eggs and stir until fully cooked and combined with the rice.
8. Add the chicken back to the pan and mix everything together.
9. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.

Why It's Great for Lean Bulking
✅ Over 50g of high-quality protein for muscle growth
✅ High-calorie meal ideal for weight gain
✅ Rice replenishes glycogen stores after workouts
✅ Eggs provide additional protein and healthy fats
✅ One-pan meal that's easy to prepare and meal-prep friendly
