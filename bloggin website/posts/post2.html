power oats bowl

🍱 break-fast 📆 08-jun-2026 💪 high protein
Nutrition (Approx.)

Calories: 700-750 kcal
Protein: 40-45g
Carbs: 80-90g
Fats: 20-25g

Perfect for muscle gain and clean bulking.

ingredients
80g rolled oats, 250ml milk (or low-fat milk), 1 medium banana, 2 whole eggs, 3 egg whites, 1 tbsp peanut butter, 1 tbsp honey, A pinch of cinnamon (optional).

Instructions
1. Cook oats with milk for 5 minutes.
2. Slice the banana and mix it into the oats.
3. Stir in peanut butter and honey.
4. Scramble 2 whole eggs and 3 egg whites separately.
5. Serve eggs on the side and enjoy.

Why It's Great for Lean Bulking
✅ High-quality protein for muscle growth
✅ Slow-digesting carbs for sustained energy
✅ Healthy fats support hormones and recovery
✅ Keeps you full for hours
